EQUIS SAR MVP

Upload your SAR document to create a workspace

⚠️ Privacy Notice:

This is a development MVP. Workspaces are public by link and accessible to anyone with the workspace ID. Do NOT upload documents containing sensitive or personally identifiable information (PII). All files are stored locally on this server without authentication or encryption.

Only .docx and .doc files are accepted

How it works:

  1. Upload your SAR document (.docx or .doc format)
  2. The system extracts and analyzes the document structure
  3. Sections are automatically detected based on headings and formatting
  4. You'll receive a unique workspace link to view and edit your document
  5. Changes are saved to your workspace automatically
