# EQUIS SAR Generator - User Guide

**For Non-Technical Users**

This guide explains how to use the EQUIS SAR Generator to create your 2026 Self-Assessment Report.

## What This Tool Does

The EQUIS SAR Generator helps you:
1. Upload your previous 2022 SAR and evidence files
2. Automatically generate a 2026 SAR draft with the same structure
3. Get AI-powered suggestions for improvements
4. Link evidence to each section automatically
5. See which EQUIS standards are covered
6. Export a polished Word document

## Getting Started

### Step 1: Launch the Application

1. Open the application (your IT team should provide the URL or launch it for you)
2. You'll see a welcome screen with a purple background
3. Read the banner message that explains how the tool works

### Step 2: Upload Your Previous SAR

1. **Click the upload area** or **drag and drop** your 2022 SAR file
2. Accepted formats: Word (.docx), PDF, or text files
3. You'll see your file name and size displayed
4. Click **"Continue"** to proceed

**Tip:** Make sure you're uploading your complete SAR, not just a section.

### Step 3: Upload Evidence Files

1. **Click the upload area** or **drag and drop** multiple files at once
2. Accepted formats: PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, or images
3. You'll see a list of all uploaded files with their sizes
4. You can remove files by clicking the **×** button
5. Click **"Continue"** when done

**Tip:** Upload all relevant evidence files. The system will automatically match them to your SAR sections.

### Step 4: Generate Your Draft

1. Review the summary showing your SAR and evidence count
2. Click **"Generate Draft"**
3. Wait while the system analyzes your files (this takes 1-2 minutes)
4. You'll automatically be taken to the editor when ready

## Using the Editor

### Understanding the Three-Panel Layout

**Left Panel - Section Navigator**
- Shows all sections from your SAR
- Colored dots indicate coverage quality:
  - 🟢 Green = Good coverage (>70%)
  - 🟡 Yellow = Fair coverage (40-70%)
  - 🔴 Red = Needs improvement (<40%)
- Orange badges show number of suggestions per section
- Click any section to edit it

**Middle Panel - Editor**
- Shows section title and coverage score
- Large text area for editing content
- "Show Suggestions" toggle to view AI recommendations
- "Save Changes" button to save your edits

**Right Panel - Evidence**
- Shows evidence files linked to the current section
- Confidence levels (High/Medium/Low)
- Brief excerpts from matching evidence
- "Insert Citation" button to add references

### Working with Suggestions

#### Viewing Suggestions

1. Click the **"Show Suggestions"** checkbox in the editor header
2. A panel appears below the editor showing all AI suggestions
3. Each suggestion includes:
   - Type (insertion, replacement, deletion)
   - The suggested change
   - Why it's being suggested
   - Related EQUIS standard (with exact clause text)
   - Confidence level

#### Accepting or Rejecting Suggestions

For each suggestion, you can:
- Click **"Accept"** (green checkmark) - The content will be automatically updated
- Click **"Reject"** (red X) - The suggestion will be marked as rejected

After accepting/rejecting suggestions:
1. Review the updated content in the editor
2. Make any manual edits if needed
3. Click **"Save Changes"**

**Tip:** Accept high-confidence suggestions first. Review low-confidence ones carefully.

#### Understanding Suggestion Types

- **INSERTION**: Add new text (usually at the end of a section)
- **REPLACEMENT**: Change existing text to new text
- **DELETION**: Remove outdated or incorrect text

### Gaps and Warnings

If you see a yellow box labeled "Identified Gaps," it means:
- Missing evidence references
- Insufficient quantitative data
- No clear link to EQUIS standards
- Section needs more work

**Action:** Address these gaps by editing content or uploading additional evidence.

## Checking Coverage

### Viewing the Coverage Dashboard

1. Click **"Coverage"** in the top navigation bar
2. You'll see:
   - Overall coverage percentage (aim for >70%)
   - Breakdown by each EQUIS standard
   - Missing criteria that need attention
   - Priority action items

### Understanding the Dashboard

**Overall Coverage Card**
- Shows your total EQUIS coverage as a percentage
- Green (>70%) = Good
- Yellow (40-70%) = Needs improvement
- Red (<40%) = Requires significant work

**Standards Breakdown**
- Each EQUIS standard has its own card
- Shows how many criteria are covered
- Lists missing criteria you need to address
- Color-coded progress bars

**Priority Action Items**
- Numbered list of what to work on first
- Focus on standards below 70% coverage
- Address missing criteria with new evidence

## Exporting Your SAR

### Creating a Word Document

1. Click the **download icon** (⬇) in the top bar
2. A dialog appears with export options:
   - **Include evidence footnotes** ✓ (Recommended: ON)
   - **Include inline suggestions** (Your choice)
   - **Include suggestions annex** (Shows all AI decisions)
3. Click **"Export"**
4. Your browser will download: `EQUIS_SAR_2026_Draft.docx`

### Export Options Explained

**Include evidence footnotes**
- Adds citations like "[See: Evidence_File.pdf]" in your document
- **Recommended:** Keep this ON for transparency

**Include inline suggestions**
- Shows all AI suggestions as tracked changes in Word
- Useful if you want to review suggestions in Word instead
- **Recommended:** OFF if you've already reviewed suggestions in the app

**Include suggestions annex**
- Adds an appendix showing all suggestions and your decisions (accepted/rejected)
- Useful for documentation and audit trail
- **Recommended:** ON for institutional records

### After Export

1. Open the downloaded Word file
2. Review formatting and content
3. Make final manual edits as needed
4. Share with your team for review
5. Finalize for submission

## Ending Your Session

### Why Delete Your Session?

This tool does NOT save your data permanently. When you're done:

1. Click the **trash icon** (🗑️) in the top bar
2. Confirm deletion
3. All uploaded files and generated content are removed

**Important:** Export your DOCX before deleting the session!

### Privacy Note

- The app treats all your SAR and evidence as confidential
- Nothing is stored after you delete the session
- Each time you use the app, you'll start fresh

## Common Questions

**Q: Can I save my work and come back later?**
A: No, sessions are temporary. Complete your work in one sitting and export before ending the session.

**Q: What if I upload the wrong file?**
A: Delete the session and start over. There's no edit function for uploaded files.

**Q: Can I add evidence after generating the draft?**
A: Not currently. Upload all evidence in Step 2 before generating.

**Q: What if my PDF is scanned (images)?**
A: The system will try OCR (text recognition), but results may vary. Use text-based PDFs when possible.

**Q: How accurate are the EQUIS standard matches?**
A: The system uses AI to match content semantically. Review suggestions carefully and use your judgment.

**Q: Can I edit the suggested text before accepting?**
A: Yes! Accept the suggestion, then manually edit the content in the editor before saving.

**Q: What does "Coverage 0%" mean?**
A: The section doesn't clearly match any EQUIS criteria. Review and add relevant content.

**Q: Should I accept all suggestions?**
A: No. Use your institutional knowledge. Accept what makes sense, reject what doesn't fit.

**Q: Can multiple people use this at once?**
A: This is a single-user app. Each person needs their own session.

**Q: Where is my EQUIS Standards PDF?**
A: The system uses a loaded EQUIS Standards document. You don't need to upload it.
