Financial Reporting Requirements for Annual Reports

This document outlines the mandatory financial information that must be included in all annual reports.

Required Financial Metrics:

1. Revenue Information:
   - Total revenue for the fiscal year
   - Revenue breakdown by business segment or product line
   - Year-over-year revenue growth percentage
   - Revenue per customer or geographic region

2. Profitability Metrics:
   - Gross profit and gross profit margin
   - Operating profit/EBITDA
   - Net profit after taxes
   - Earnings per share (EPS)

3. Balance Sheet Information:
   - Total assets and asset breakdown
   - Total liabilities and debt levels
   - Shareholder equity
   - Cash and cash equivalents

4. Cash Flow:
   - Operating cash flow
   - Investing cash flow
   - Financing cash flow
   - Free cash flow

5. Financial Ratios:
   - Return on equity (ROE)
   - Return on assets (ROA)
   - Debt-to-equity ratio
   - Current ratio and quick ratio

6. Cost Analysis:
   - Cost of goods sold (COGS)
   - Operating expenses breakdown
   - R&D expenses
   - Sales and marketing expenses

7. Future Financial Outlook:
   - Revenue projections for next fiscal year
   - Expected profit margins
   - Planned capital expenditures
   - Financial risks and mitigation strategies
